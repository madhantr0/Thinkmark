Process

How ThinkMark Works

Simple 4-step process. Teacher sets up once before exam — then just swipe and done for every student paper.

01
Upload Question Paper
Teacher uploads question paper and answer key to ThinkMark web dashboard before the exam begins.
02
Swipe Answer Sheet
Teacher swipes student answer sheet through the T-shaped IoT scanner. Like a Dmart billing scanner — but for exam papers.
03
AI Evaluates
Google Gemini AI reads handwriting — Tamil or English — evaluates theory, math equations, graphs and diagrams against answer key.
04
Marks in 4 Seconds
Marks, grade, pass/fail result and class ranking appear instantly on any phone browser. No app needed.

Features

What Makes ThinkMark Unique

T-Shape IoT Hardware
First physical IoT scanner device for exam grading. Arduino + ESP8266 WiFi module. No other competitor has hardware.
Tamil Language Support
Only exam grader in India with Tamil handwriting support. Uses Sarvam AI — built specifically for Indian languages.
Math & Graph Evaluation
Reads handwritten math equations, drawn graphs, chemical equations and diagrams — not just plain text answers.
Works on Any Browser
No app installation. Open any browser on any phone or laptop. Just connect to device WiFi and start grading.
Student-Proof Security
JWT auth, rate limiting, duplicate scan blocking. Marks calculated only on backend. Students cannot hack or change marks.
₹3,999 Total Cost
Eklavvya costs ₹4.5 lakh per year. ThinkMark device costs ₹3,999 one time. 100x cheaper. Accessible to every school.
